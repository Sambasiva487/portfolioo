research-led redesign

Graduate assistantship: redesigned student-facing pages on the university website using usability findings, responsive design, and wcag grounding, not a facelift, a research-backed simplification.

Role
UX/UI Designer (Graduate Assistant)
Timeline
Feb 2025, May 2025
Stack
Wireframes · Usability testing · Responsive
Scope

Work + findings

The research context meant I could tie every redesign decision to actual usability findings: real student session data shaped information architecture.

  • Responsive layouts that preserved hierarchy from 1440px down to 375px without truncating tasks.
  • Usability tests informed navigation simplification and content hierarchy for high-traffic student pages.
